About MGMC

Motion Graphics Music Cutter (MGMC) is a small in-browser tool to cut music for motion graphics. It runs entirely locally — your audio never leaves your machine.

How it works: import an audio file, trim the region you want, shape the lowpass cutoff envelope (drag points, bend the curve, right-click a point to toggle linear), place beat markers, then export the audio clip and the timestamp list.

Controls: Space play / pause · A place a marker · Del remove the selected marker · Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z undo / redo · scroll to zoom · Shift+scroll to pan.
